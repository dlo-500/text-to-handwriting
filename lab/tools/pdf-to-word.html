PDF to Word

Pulls the text out of a PDF into an editable .docx, guessing headings from font size and bullets from list markers. Built for getting the words back, not for pixel-perfect layout — tables, columns and exact spacing won't carry over.

🔒 Runs on your device — nothing is uploaded
